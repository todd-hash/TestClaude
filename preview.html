Does Any of This Sound Familiar?

Most families come to me when they've been worrying in silence for months — juggling caregiving, jobs, and the weight of a decision no one wants to make. Here's what I hear most:

"I don't know where to start."

The house is full of a lifetime of memories. The idea of sorting through everything — let alone selling — feels paralyzing. You're not sure what to do first, second, or at all.

"I'm worried about Mom's safety."

She says she's fine, but you've noticed the signs. The house is too much for her now. You need a plan that honors her dignity while keeping her safe — and you needed it yesterday.

"I'm afraid of making the wrong decision."

This is likely the most significant move of your parent's life. The stakes feel enormous, and you're scared of rushing, choosing wrong, or missing something important.

"I feel like I'm doing this alone."

Siblings have opinions. Parents have fears. Agents talk about comps. Nobody is talking about the whole picture — the emotional, financial, and logistical reality all at once.
